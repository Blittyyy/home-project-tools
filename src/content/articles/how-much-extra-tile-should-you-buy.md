There is no single extra-tile percentage that fits every installation. Around **10%** is commonly suggested for straightforward layouts, while diagonal or more cut-heavy work is often planned closer to **15%**, but product, layout, room shape, and future-repair needs can change the answer.

An editable allowance is more useful than automatically adding the same percentage to every tile job. That is how the FixUpWise [Tile Calculator](/calculators/tile-calculator) is built: **Straight → 10%**, **Diagonal → 15%**, or **Custom**.

## Why extra tile is purchased

- Perimeter and obstacle cuts  
- Breakage while cutting or handling  
- Layout inefficiency (offcuts that do not reuse well)  
- Matching material later (“attic stock”)  

## Straight / simple layouts

Supplier planning discussions often start near **10%** for standard straight or simple offset work (Clé, Bedrosians, Merola). That is a planning band, not a guarantee for every room.

## Diagonal / more cut-heavy layouts

Clé and Bedrosians discuss about **15%** for diagonal or more intricate patterns. Merola notes about **15%** when rooms have many jogs and corners. Angled perimeter cuts create more unused triangles — one reason the calculator suggests a higher default for diagonal.

## Complex patterns and irregular rooms

Herringbone, many niches, or elaborate patterns can need still more. Merola points to installer judgment for elaborate work. Bedrosians notes higher overage for complex patterns beyond a simple diagonal.

## Spare tile for future repairs

Daltile discusses purchasing about **10%** more as attic stock for repairs and dye-lot matching. Do not automatically stack a second attic-stock percentage on top of the calculator waste field. If your supplier separates layout waste from attic stock, choose one combined percentage that matches how you intend to order.

These are supplier planning discussions — not a free TCNA universal waste table.

## Decision checklist

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Straight / simple rectangle | Common ~10% planning band |
| Diagonal layout | Common ~15% planning band |
| Many jogs / irregular room | Higher allowance; product/installer guidance |
| Complex pattern (e.g. herringbone) | Often above the simple diagonal band |
| Future dye-lot matching | Spares from the same order |

`tiles to buy = ceil(rawExact × (1 + waste%))`
