Two coats are a useful estimating default, but they are not a universal rule. The number of coats depends on the paint, surface, color change, primer, and finish you are trying to achieve.

For estimating, an editable coat count is more useful than promising that one-coat or two-coat coverage applies to every wall. The FixUpWise [Paint Calculator](/calculators/paint-calculator) starts at **2** coats for that reason — change the field to match your plan.

## When two coats are commonly appropriate

Manufacturer signals that often point toward two finish coats:

- Benjamin Moore’s online calculator **assumes two coats** for its rough estimate
- Some Benjamin Moore product sheets (for example Regal Select) recommend **two coats for full color development** on that product
- Large color changes, uneven previous finishes, or a goal of uniform sheen

## When one coat may be sufficient

- Similar color over a sound, previously painted wall
- A product labeled or tested for one-coat hide in specified colors — still follow that product’s instructions
- A proper primer system that improves hide before the finish coat

One coat is a project decision supported by the product and surface, not a shortcut that applies to every wall.

## Situations that may require more work

Behr’s interior buying guidance notes additional coats (or more paint) may be needed for porous, rough, textured, previously unpainted surfaces, or dramatic color changes. Sherwin-Williams notes requirements vary with application method and surface condition.

Bare drywall, heavy repairs, and strong hue shifts often mean primer plus more than a single finish coat.

## How coat count changes quantity

`gallons = paintable area × coats ÷ coverage`

Doubling coats doubles coated square footage at the same coverage. Purchase rounding can still jump by a full gallon.

Primer is not a substitute “coat of paint” in the wall-paint field — estimate primer with its own coverage if needed.

## Product instructions beat generic rules

The label and technical data for the paint you buy outrank any site-wide default. Coverage rates that feed the same formula are covered in [how many square feet a gallon covers](/materials/how-many-square-feet-does-a-gallon-of-paint-cover).
