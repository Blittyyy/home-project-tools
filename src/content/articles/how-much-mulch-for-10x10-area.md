A **10 × 10 ft** rectangle is **100 sq ft**. Mulch volume still depends on depth:

`volume (cu ft) = 100 × (depth_inches ÷ 12)`  
`cubic yards = cu ft ÷ 27`

The depths below are **quantity examples** aligned with common planning shortcuts and a depth band often discussed in extension guidance. They are not a recommendation for your plants or soil. For nuance, see [How Deep Should Mulch Be?](/guides/how-deep-should-mulch-be).

Bag counts use `ceil(cubic feet ÷ 2)` — the same rounding as the [Mulch Calculator](/calculators/mulch-calculator). Optional extra material defaults to 0% in these examples.

## Depth changes quantity substantially

Moving from 2 in to 4 in on the same 10×10 footprint nearly doubles cubic feet (16.67 → 33.33) and jumps from **9** to **17** bags at 2 cu ft each. Measure the bed and choose depth intentionally before you shop.

Irregular beds, circles, or known square footage use the same volume math — enter them in the calculator or follow [How Much Mulch Do I Need?](/projects/how-much-mulch-do-i-need).
