A **10 × 10 ft** area is **100 sq ft**. At **2 inches**, that is about **0.62 yd³** — **9** of the **2-cu-ft** bags.

A “10×10 mulch estimate” without a depth is incomplete. A common mistake is giving one bag count for 100 sq ft without stating the assumed depth.

## The 100-sq-ft area

**10 ft × 10 ft = 100 sq ft**

Volume still depends on depth:

`cu ft = 100 × (inches ÷ 12)`  
`yd³ = cu ft ÷ 27`

General formula: [how much mulch do I need?](/projects/how-much-mulch-do-i-need).

## Worked example at 2 inches

Depth in feet: 2 ÷ 12 ≈ **0.1667 ft**

**100 × 0.1667 ≈ 16.67 cu ft**  
**16.67 ÷ 27 ≈ 0.62 yd³**  
**ceil(16.67 ÷ 2) = 9** bags at 2 cu ft

Bag counts use `ceil(cu ft ÷ 2)` — the same rounding as the [Mulch Calculator](/calculators/mulch-calculator). No overage included.

## Why depth changes the answer

Moving from 2 in to 4 in on the same footprint nearly doubles cubic feet (**16.67 → 33.33**) and jumps from **9** to **17** bags at 2 cu ft each. Choose depth intentionally before you shop.
