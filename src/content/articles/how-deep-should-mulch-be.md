**No single depth fits every bed.** Depth depends on the mulch material, what is already on the ground, soil drainage, weed-suppression goals, moisture needs, and whether you are mulching annual beds, shrubs, or trees.

## What extension guidance commonly says

Several university extension and arboricultural sources discuss roughly **2–4 inches** of mulch for many landscape and tree applications:

- University of Illinois Extension recommends about **2–4 inches**, with composted materials often in a **2–3 inch** band and coarser mulches **3–4 inches**, and mulch kept off the trunk.
- University of Vermont Extension describes about **2–4 inches** in a flat ring, not a mound against the bark.
- Wisconsin Horticulture notes **2–4 inches**, often deeper on light well-drained soils and shallower on heavier clay.
- University of Maryland Extension emphasizes keeping mulch **shallow** (often about **1–3 inches** depending on material) and off the trunk.

These are planning ranges from horticultural guidance — not a FixUpWise rule and not a substitute for local advice when plants are stressed or conditions are unusual.

## Keep mulch off trunks and stems

Do **not** pile mulch against tree trunks or shrub stems (“mulch volcanoes”). Extension and ISA guidance consistently warn that mulch against bark can trap moisture, invite pests, and hide the root flare. Pull mulch back so the trunk/root flare stays clear. This article is quantity and depth planning — not tree diagnosis or treatment.

## How depth changes how much you buy

Coverage per cubic yard falls as depth rises:

| Depth | Coverage per cubic yard |
| --- | --- |
| 2 in | 162 sq ft |
| 3 in | 108 sq ft |
| 4 in | 81 sq ft |

Formula: `27 ÷ depth_in_feet`. Full table (including 1 in as math only): [How Much Area Does a Cubic Yard of Mulch Cover?](/materials/how-much-area-does-a-yard-of-mulch-cover).

Example: a **100 sq ft** bed needs about **0.62 yd³** at 2 in, **0.93 yd³** at 3 in, and **1.23 yd³** at 4 in — same footprint, different depth. See [How Much Mulch Do I Need?](/projects/how-much-mulch-do-i-need).
