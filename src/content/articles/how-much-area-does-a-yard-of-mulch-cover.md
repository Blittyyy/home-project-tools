One cubic yard of mulch covers:

| Depth | Sq ft per yd³ |
| --- | --- |
| **1 in** | **324** |
| **2 in** | **162** |
| **3 in** | **108** |
| **4 in** | **81** |

“Coverage per yard” is meaningless without stating depth. **1 inch** is mathematical coverage only — not a blanket landscape recommendation.

## Formula

**1 yd³ = 27 cu ft**

`coverage (sq ft) = 27 ÷ depth in feet`

At 2 in: depth = 2/12 ft → `27 ÷ (2/12) = 162 sq ft`.

## Why depth changes coverage

Thicker mulch uses more volume on the same footprint, so each yard stretches across fewer square feet. That is pure geometry — separate from horticultural depth guidance in [how deep should mulch be?](/guides/how-deep-should-mulch-be).

## Quick worked example

**100 sq ft** at **2 in**:

`100 ÷ 162 ≈ 0.62 yd³`

Same result as area × depth ÷ 27 in [how much mulch do I need?](/projects/how-much-mulch-do-i-need).

## Using this when ordering bulk

1. Choose finish depth  
2. Read coverage from the table (or compute `27 ÷ depth_ft`)  
3. `yards ≈ area_sq_ft ÷ coverage_sq_ft_per_yd³`
