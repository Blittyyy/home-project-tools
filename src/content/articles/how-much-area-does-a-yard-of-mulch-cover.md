**Coverage depends on depth.** One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so:

`coverage (sq ft) = 27 ÷ depth in feet`

**1 inch** is included for coverage math only. It is not presented here as a recommended landscape depth. Extension sources more often discuss roughly **2–4 inches** for many beds — see [How Deep Should Mulch Be?](/guides/how-deep-should-mulch-be).

The [Mulch Calculator](/calculators/mulch-calculator) derives `coverageSqFtPerYd3 = 27 / depthFt` from the depth you enter. It does not hardcode a single “industry” coverage number.

## Using coverage to reverse-estimate yards

`cubic yards ≈ area_sq_ft ÷ coverage_sq_ft_per_yd³`

Example: **100 sq ft** at **2 in** → `100 ÷ 162 ≈ 0.62 yd³`, matching the worked example in [How Much Mulch Do I Need?](/projects/how-much-mulch-do-i-need).

Uneven beds, paths mixed with planting areas, or leftover mulch already in place still need measured area and a chosen finish depth — coverage tables alone do not replace that.
