**Mulch volume** =

**area × depth**

For a rectangle:

**area = length × width**

Convert depth to feet (inches ÷ 12), multiply for **cubic feet**, then divide by **27** for **cubic yards**.

For mulch estimates, depth usually changes the answer more dramatically than small differences in bed dimensions.

A common mistake is calculating square footage correctly but forgetting that mulch is purchased by **volume**.

<aside class="planning-note" role="note">
  <p class="planning-note__title">Area is only half the estimate</p>
  <p class="planning-note__body">100 sq ft does not tell you the mulch quantity until depth is included. Measure the bed first and choose the intended depth before deciding whether to buy bags or bulk.</p>
</aside>

## What to measure

- Bed **length** and **width** (or known area / diameter for circles)
- Intended finish **depth**
- Package volume if buying bags (**cu ft** on the label)
- Whether you want any optional extra-material allowance (defaults to **0%** in the calculator)

## Area × depth

1. Area in sq ft  
2. Depth in feet = inches ÷ 12  
3. Cubic feet = area × depth (ft)  
4. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27  

That is the same path used by the FixUpWise [Mulch Calculator](/calculators/mulch-calculator) for rectangle, circle, and known-area modes.

## Cubic feet → cubic yards

**1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet**

Example: **10 × 10 ft** at **2 in**

- Area = 100 sq ft  
- Depth = 2 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.1667 ft  
- Volume ≈ **16.67 cu ft**  
- Yards ≈ **0.62 yd³**

## Bags vs bulk

Bag counts divide purchase volume by bag size and round **up**. The calculator compares common U.S. package volumes of **1.5**, **2**, and **3 cu ft** — not every product. Read the label.

Larger jobs are often ordered as bulk by the cubic yard; suppliers set their own minimums and delivery fees. Bag math: [how many bags in a cubic yard](/materials/how-many-bags-of-mulch-in-a-cubic-yard).

## Choosing a depth

Depth is a landscape decision, not a fixed calculator rule. Extension sources often discuss roughly **2–4 inches** for common beds, with important caveats — see [how deep should mulch be?](/guides/how-deep-should-mulch-be). Keep mulch pulled back from trunks and stems when you install.

## Optional extra material

The calculator’s extra-material field defaults to **0%**. Use it only if you want a planning cushion for uneven beds or measurement variation — it is not a settling model.

## Pre-buy checklist

- Bed measured (or known area entered)
- Finish depth chosen (and existing mulch checked)
- Bags vs bulk decided from volume, not guesswork
- Bag label volume confirmed if packaging
- Trunks / stems kept clear of piled mulch
