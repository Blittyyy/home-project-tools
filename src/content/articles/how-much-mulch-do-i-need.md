Mulch quantity comes from **area** and **depth**. Depth choice is a landscape decision — not a fixed rule in this calculator — so treat the inches you enter as a planning input. Many extension sources discuss roughly **2–4 inches** for common landscape beds; see [How Deep Should Mulch Be?](/guides/how-deep-should-mulch-be).

## Rectangle formula

**Area (sq ft)** =

`length × width`

**Volume (cu ft)** =

`area × depth in feet`

(Convert inches to feet by dividing by 12.)

**Cubic yards** =

`cubic feet ÷ 27`

**Bags** =

`ceil(purchase cubic feet ÷ bag volume in cu ft)`

The FixUpWise [Mulch Calculator](/calculators/mulch-calculator) uses the same structure for rectangle, circle, and known-area modes. Optional extra material defaults to **0%**.

## Worked example: 10 × 10 ft at 2 in

- Area: `10 × 10 = 100 sq ft`
- Depth: `2 ÷ 12 ≈ 0.1667 ft`
- Volume: `100 × 0.1667 ≈ 16.67 cu ft`
- Cubic yards: `16.67 ÷ 27 ≈ 0.62 yd³`

At **2 cu ft** per bag:

`ceil(16.67 ÷ 2) = 9` bags

Keep these numbers separate:

| Result | Value (this example) |
| --- | --- |
| Volume | ≈ 16.67 cu ft |
| Cubic yards | ≈ 0.62 yd³ |
| 2 cu ft bags | 9 (rounded up) |

## Bags vs bulk

Bag sizes vary. The calculator compares common U.S. package volumes of **1.5**, **2**, and **3 cu ft** — not every product on the shelf. Read the bag label. For how many bags equal one yard, see [How Many Bags of Mulch Are in a Cubic Yard?](/materials/how-many-bags-of-mulch-in-a-cubic-yard).

Coverage per yard also depends only on depth: [How Much Area Does a Cubic Yard of Mulch Cover?](/materials/how-much-area-does-a-yard-of-mulch-cover).

## What this estimate does not cover

- Settling or decomposition over time (no universal factor in the tool)
- Price or delivery minimums for bulk mulch
- Plant health diagnosis or tree-ring design — keep mulch pulled back from trunks and stems when you install
