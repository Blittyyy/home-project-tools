## Why this page exists

This article exists so the starter can demonstrate:

- published article routes
- category hub listing
- internal search indexing
- sitemap inclusion

## What to configure first

1. Edit `src/config/site.ts` for name, domain, tagline, and author.
2. Edit `src/config/categories.ts` for your niche information architecture.
3. Rewrite review callouts in `src/config/review.ts`.
4. Review legal page shells under `src/pages/`.

## What not to copy from a previous niche

Do not bring over niche articles, brand assets, or domain-specific calculators from another site. Reuse architecture and editorial process only.
