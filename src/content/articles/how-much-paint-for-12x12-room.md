A **12 × 12 ft** room with **8-ft walls** has about **384 sq ft** of wall surface before openings.

At the FixUpWise planning default of **350 sq ft/gal**:

- **1 coat:** ≈ **1.10 gal** → buy **2** whole gallons  
- **2 coats:** ≈ **2.19 gal** → buy **3** whole gallons  

“12×12 room” is not enough information by itself. Wall height and coats can change the estimate materially. A common mistake is assuming every 12×12 room uses the same amount of paint.

## The 12×12 × 8-ft example

Gross wall area:

**2 × (12 + 12) × 8 = 384 sq ft**

Coverage used below: **350 sq ft per gallon** (editable in the calculator — not a universal product rule). Formula background: [how much paint for a room](/projects/how-much-paint-do-i-need-for-a-room).

## Effect of doors and windows

If you use the approximate count-method allowances currently used by the FixUpWise calculator (~**20 sq ft** per door, ~**15 sq ft** per window), deductions lower net wall area before coats. One door and one window is not the same deduction as a patio door and three windows.

Do not subtract an opening you are painting with the same wall estimate. Door and trim paint are separate surfaces. More detail: [doors and windows deductions](/guides/how-much-paint-to-subtract-for-doors-and-windows).

## Why another 12×12 room may differ

Same floor plan, different job:

- **9-ft walls** → 432 sq ft gross; two coats at 350 ≈ **2.47 gal** (still buy 3)
- **10-ft walls** → 480 sq ft gross; two coats at 350 ≈ **2.74 gal** (still buy 3)
- Different **coverage** on the can (see [coverage per gallon](/materials/how-many-square-feet-does-a-gallon-of-paint-cover))
- Different **coat** plan (see [two coats](/guides/do-you-need-two-coats-of-paint))
- Large or irregular openings

Whole-gallon rounding can hide height changes until the calculated amount crosses another gallon.
