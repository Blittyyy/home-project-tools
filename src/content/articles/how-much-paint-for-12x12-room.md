A **12 × 12 ft** floor plan does not fix paint quantity by itself. Wall height, openings, coats, and coverage all matter. Use the same room formula as [How Much Paint Do I Need for a Room?](/projects/how-much-paint-do-i-need-for-a-room):

`gross wall area = 2 × (length + width) × wall height`

## Example: 12×12 with 8 ft walls

Gross wall area:

`2 × (12 + 12) × 8 = 384 sq ft`

## Openings are approximate unless you measure

If you use the calculator’s count method (~**20 sq ft** per door, ~**15 sq ft** per window), deductions are planning allowances — not exact opening measurements. A room with one door and one window is not the same as a room with a patio door and three windows.

Do not subtract openings you are painting with the same wall estimate. Door and trim paint are separate products and surfaces.

## Why “two gallons for a 12×12” is unreliable

That kind of rule of thumb usually ignores height, openings, color change, texture, and the coverage printed on your can. Prefer measured walls and the product’s stated coverage.
