> **DEMO CONTENT** — Reference sample only. Not a substitute for your niche editorial guidelines.

## Before first real publish

- Complete `docs/site-factory/new-site-setup-template.md`
- Replace demo branding and legal shells
- Delete these three sample articles
- Create a researched cluster with `.cursor/commands/create-content-cluster.md`
- Require explicit approval before `.cursor/commands/publish-content-cluster.md`

## Indexing reminders

Published pages need `draft: false` and `noindex: false`, plus no unfinished markers such as research-needed language in body or sources.

## Hard stops

Stop rather than ship when search intent overlaps existing pages, primary sources are weak, unique value is thin, or niche review requirements cannot be met.
