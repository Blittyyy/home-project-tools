When estimating **wall** paint, subtract door and window openings only if those surfaces are **not** receiving the same wall paint in this estimate.

This is a wall-area deduction method — not a trim, door, or window-paint quantity guide.

When openings are unusually large or small, measuring them is better than relying on average allowances.

## Quick-count shortcut

The approximate count-method allowances currently used by the FixUpWise [Paint Calculator](/calculators/paint-calculator) are:

- **~20 sq ft per door**
- **~15 sq ft per window**

Those figures are planning shortcuts inside the calculator. They are not a surveyed average of every door and window, and they should not be treated as a verified manufacturer-published standard size for every brand.

## Exact-measurement method

For each opening you are excluding:

**opening area = width × height**

Then:

**paintable wall area = gross wall area − total opening area**

The calculator also accepts a **custom total opening area** when you prefer measured deductions over counts.

## Worked example

Room: **10 × 12 ft**, **8 ft** walls → gross wall area **352 sq ft**  
Openings: **1 door + 2 windows** (count method)

Approximate deduction:

**20 + 15 + 15 = 50 sq ft**

Net wall area:

**352 − 50 = 302 sq ft**

At **2 coats** and **350 sq ft/gal**:

**302 × 2 = 604** coated sq ft  
**604 ÷ 350 ≈ 1.73 gal** → **2** whole gallons

Without openings, the same room was about **2.01** calculated gallons → **3** whole gallons. Full room formula: [how much paint for a room](/projects/how-much-paint-do-i-need-for-a-room).

## When not to subtract an opening

- You are painting the door face or sash **as part of the same wall-paint estimate** (unusual; usually separate products)
- You want a slightly conservative overestimate and prefer to leave openings in
- The “opening” is still painted as wall surface (for example a wide pass-through treated as wall)

## Common mistakes

- Subtracting a door or window from the wall estimate and then treating that same subtraction as if it already bought paint for the door or trim — those are different paint tasks
- Using the 20 / 15 shortcuts for a patio door, picture window, or other nonstandard opening without measuring
- Deducting glass area from wall paint and forgetting trim still needs its own product

| Surface | Typical treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Walls | Wall paint estimate; may deduct openings |
| Doors | Separate door/enamel estimate if painting doors |
| Trim / casings | Separate trim paint |
