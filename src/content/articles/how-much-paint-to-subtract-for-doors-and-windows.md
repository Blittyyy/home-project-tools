When estimating **wall** paint, subtract door and window openings only if those surfaces are **not** receiving the same wall paint in this estimate. The deduction reduces paintable wall area before you apply coats and coverage.

This article is about **wall-area deduction only**. It is not a trim, door, or window-paint quantity guide.

## Two methods

### A. Quick count method (planning shortcut)

The FixUpWise [Paint Calculator](/calculators/paint-calculator) supports approximate allowances:

- **~20 sq ft per door**
- **~15 sq ft per window**

These are estimating shortcuts used for planning — not a survey of every door and window size. Manufacturer tools and contractor rules of thumb sometimes use slightly different standard sizes; when openings are large, irregular, or critical to the buy, measure instead.

### B. Measured openings (more accurate)

For each opening:

`opening area = width × height`

Add the openings you are excluding, then:

`paintable wall area = gross wall area − total opening area`

The Paint Calculator also accepts a **custom total opening area** when you prefer measured deductions over counts.

## Worked example (count method)

Room: **10 × 12 ft**, **8 ft** walls → gross wall area **352 sq ft**  
Openings: **1 door + 2 windows**

Approximate deduction:

`20 + 15 + 15 = 50 sq ft`

Net wall area:

`352 − 50 = 302 sq ft`

At **2 coats** and **350 sq ft/gal**:

`302 × 2 ÷ 350 ≈ 1.73` calculated gallons → **2 whole gallons** purchase

Without openings, the same room was about **2.01** calculated gallons → **3** whole gallons. See [How Much Paint Do I Need for a Room?](/projects/how-much-paint-do-i-need-for-a-room).

## When not to subtract

- You are painting the door face or window sash **as part of the same wall-paint estimate** (unusual; usually separate products/colors)
- You want a slightly conservative overestimate and prefer to skip deductions
- The “opening” is a pass-through you are still painting as wall surface

## Do not mix wall, trim, and door paint

| Surface | Typical treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Walls | Wall paint estimate; may deduct openings |
| Doors | Separate door/enamel estimate if painting doors |
| Trim / casings | Separate trim paint; usually not “subtracted” from wall gallons as a finish product |

Subtracting an opening from wall area avoids buying wall paint for glass and door slabs. It does **not** automatically create a gallon estimate for painting the door or trim.
