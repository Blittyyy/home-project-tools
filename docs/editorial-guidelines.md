# Editorial Guidelines (Template)

Rewrite this file for `[NICHE]` before publishing real content.

## Voice

- Direct answers near the top
- Plain language
- No invented credentials or statistics

## Review / risk boundaries

Align with `src/config/review.ts`:

- When `none` is allowed
- When `general` / `caution` callouts are required
- When `professional` help must be recommended
- DIY steps that are forbidden for this niche

## Sources

Allowed:

- Primary standards, official docs, government/agency guidance (niche-specific list)

Not allowed as factual authorities:

- Forums and social threads
- Affiliate listicles
- AI summaries
- Competitor SEO pages (structure only)
